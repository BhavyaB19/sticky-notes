# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 30, 2026_

Sticky Notes is a browser extension for putting notes on webpages. It is built so that your notes stay with you.

## What the extension stores

- **Your notes**: the text, color, size and position of each note, plus the address and title of the page it belongs to.
- **Your settings**: the list of sites where you chose to hide notes.

All of this is saved in your browser's own extension storage (`storage.local`) on your device. It is never sent anywhere.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not send your notes, your browsing activity or anything else to the developer or to any server. The extension makes no network requests.
- It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
- It does not read or collect the content of the pages you visit. It only reads a page's address and title, to know which notes belong there.
- It does not sell or share data, because it has none.

## Backups

The "Export" button on the All notes page saves your notes to a JSON file on your computer. That file is yours, and the extension doesn't upload it anywhere.

## Permissions

- **Access to all websites**: needed so notes can appear on any page you choose to put them on.
- **storage**: saves your notes on your device.
- **contextMenus**: adds "Add sticky note here" to the right-click menu.
- **activeTab**: lets the toolbar popup and keyboard shortcut know which page you're on.
- **search**: the search box on the new tab page hands your query to your browser's default search engine. The extension never sees the results or keeps your searches.

## Removing your data

Delete notes individually, or use "Delete all notes" on the All notes page. Uninstalling the extension also removes everything it stored.
